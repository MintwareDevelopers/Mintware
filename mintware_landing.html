How it works

Three steps to earning what you deserve

Mintware uses Attribution to read your on-chain history and make sure rewards flow to the wallets that have earned them.

01 🔍

Your history gets scored

Attribution reads your full on-chain history — LP positions, DeFi activity, wallet age, referral network — and computes your score from 0 to 100.

02 ⚡

Score weights your rewards

Inside Mintware vaults and reward pools, your Attribution score determines your share of fee distributions, referral rewards, and token team distributions.

03 📈

Reputation compounds over time

Every action you take on-chain improves your score. Refer strong wallets, provide liquidity, stay active — your reputation grows and your earnings follow.
